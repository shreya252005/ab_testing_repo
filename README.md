# ab_testing_repo

Checkout Optimization: A/B Testing & Statistical Analysis📌 Project OverviewAn end-to-end A/B testing simulation and statistical analysis pipeline built to evaluate the business impact of replacing an interactive stadium seating map with a streamlined "Quick Select" modal on a high-volume ticketing marketplace.This project demonstrates rigorous experimental design, data validation, hypothesis testing, and advanced data visualization to support data-driven product decisions.📊 Methodology & Statistical RigorThe analysis follows a structured data science workflow to ensure high statistical power and avoid common experimentation pitfalls:Experimental Design & Simulation: Simulated $50,000$ user sessions split evenly (50/50 allocation) into Control (Interactive Map) and Variant (Quick Select) groups using pseudo-random hashing.Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM) Validation: Executed a Chi-Square Goodness-of-Fit Test to verify traffic allocation integrity and rule out technical bias ($p > 0.05$).Hypothesis Testing: Utilized a Two-Proportion Z-Test to measure conversion rate lift.Uncertainty Quantification: Calculated 95% Wilson Score Intervals to accurately capture proportion confidence boundaries.Bootstrapping: Performed $1,000$ bootstrap resamples to generate a distribution of relative lift and visualize outcome probabilities.📈 Key Findings & VisualizationsThe analysis pipeline generates three professional analytical charts to evaluate the experiment:Conversion Rate Comparison: Demonstrates the point estimate lift backed by 95% Wilson Confidence Intervals.Cumulative Conversion Convergence: Tracks running conversion averages over a simulated 14-day window to guard against early-stopping errors.Bootstrap Lift Distribution: Illustrates the spread of relative lift, providing a clear picture of statistical confidence.Business Takeaway: The "Quick Select" variant yielded a statistically significant increase in checkout conversion rate (rising from 5.20% to 5.70%, $p < 0.05$), directly reducing choice paralysis and improving transaction efficiency without introducing traffic bias.🛠️ Tech StackLanguage: PythonLibraries: pandas, numpy, scipy, statsmodels, matplotlib, seaborn🚀 How to RunYou can run, inspect, or modify the complete analysis interactively by clicking the "Open in Colab" badge at the top of this readme.
